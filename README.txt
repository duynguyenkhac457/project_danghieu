PORSCHE WEBSITE DEMO - BAN NANG CAP

CACH CHAY
1. Giai nen file ZIP.
2. Mo thu muc porsche-web-nang-cap bang VS Code.
3. Cai Live Server neu chua co.
4. Click chuot phai index.html -> Open with Live Server.

CAC TRANG
- index.html: trang chu, tim kiem, loc gia, sap xep, gio hang.
- login.html / register.html: dang nhap va dang ky.
- testdrive.html: dat lich lai thu.
- checkout.html: nhap thong tin bat buoc va hien thi QR.
- success.html: trang ghi nhan thanh cong.
- history.html: lich su mua theo tai khoan tren trinh duyet.
- porsche-*.html: 6 trang chi tiet xe.
- qr-cua-tao.png: file mau de thay bang anh QR that cua ban.

THAY ANH QR
Thay file qr-cua-tao.png bang anh QR cua ban va giu nguyen ten file.
Trong ban nay tep placeholder dang la hinh minh hoa, khong phai ma QR thanh toan co the quet.

LUU Y QUAN TRONG
- Day la website front-end demo. Tai khoan, gio hang, don hang va lich dat lai thu duoc luu trong localStorage tren trinh duyet.
- Mat khau khong duoc ma hoa va khong an toan cho website that.
- Nut xac nhan chi ghi nhan khach hang da xac nhan chuyen khoan; KHONG tu dong kiem tra tien vao tai khoan ngan hang.
- Muon ban hang that can backend, co so du lieu, bao mat va ket noi cong thanh toan/webhook.
- Gia va thong so xe chi mang tinh minh hoa, khong phai bao gia chinh thuc.
